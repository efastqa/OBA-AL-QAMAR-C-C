About Us

Welcome to AL QAMAR CENTRAL COLLEGE

We are a dedicated educational institution committed to academic excellence and holistic development. Our mission is to nurture responsible, confident, and compassionate individuals.

With a rich history and a vibrant community, we strive to create a supportive environment where every student can thrive.

Our Programs

Academics

Strong curriculum from primary to secondary level, focusing on STEM, languages, and arts.

Sports

Encouraging physical fitness and teamwork through cricket, football, athletics, and more.

Arts & Culture

Celebrating creativity with music, dance, drama, and cultural events throughout the year.

Community

Instilling values of service and leadership through community engagement and social projects.
